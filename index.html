About sizing

SizeMate uses the standard conversion charts between US, UK, China and EU sizing. Garments still vary between brands, and Chinese sizes usually run smaller than Western ones. Where the app shows body measurements in centimetres, trust those first.

Your data

Your name, country and saved sizes are kept on this phone. Sizes you share, and chat messages, are stored on the SizeMate server so the other person can see them.
